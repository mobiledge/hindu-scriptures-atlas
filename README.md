# Hindu Scriptures Atlas

An interactive, single-page atlas of 79 Hindu scriptures, from the Rigveda (c. 1500 BCE) to modern works.

Each row is a text, nested under the scripture it belongs to or comments on, with a bar showing its approximate period of composition on a shared timeline. Collapsed rows show a whole family on one line: the root text plus a mark for each part, commentary and retelling.

- **Four classes:** Shruti, Smriti, Darshana, and Agama, Tantra & bhakti
- **Solid vs. outlined bars:** scripture vs. commentary, retelling or translation
- **Year cursor:** hover the timeline to read a year and see which texts were being composed
- **Search** by text, author or idea; **detail panel** with descriptions, sections, key ideas and Wikipedia links

Open `index.html` in a browser; it has no build step or dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

A companion to [philosophy-timeline](https://github.com/mobiledge/philosophy-timeline).

Dates are approximate scholarly ranges for each text's main period of composition, and many are contested.
